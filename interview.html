Interview Preparation

Standards-based practice. Study the frameworks, then work through the flashcards.

Frameworks to study

  • EYLF v2.0 (Early Years Learning Framework) — 5 outcomes: Identity; Community; Wellbeing; Learning; Communication. Approved learning frameworks.
  • NQS — 7 quality areas (program & practice; health & safety; physical environment; staffing; relationships with children; partnerships with families; governance). National Quality Standard.
  • APST (Australian Professional Standards for Teachers, used by NESA for ECT accreditation). Teaching standards.
  • ECA Code of Ethics. ECA Code of Ethics.
  • Child protection / mandatory reporting NSW. Mandatory Reporter Guide.
