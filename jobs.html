Jobs & Employment

The path from training to a full-time role, plus where to find work.

Path to a job

The training → casual → full-time ladder:

  1. Working With Children Check — free volunteer check covers student placements; upgrade to the paid check (~$105, 5 yrs) once employed (who needs a check). Do first, together with first aid incl. asthma & anaphylaxis (e.g. HLTAID012): apply here.
  2. Enrol fee-free Cert III → mandatory placement (~160 hrs) doubles as a working interview; many students are hired casually by their placement centre.
  3. NSW ECEC Scholarship Program 2026 — ~2,000 scholarships over two rounds (up to $27M); Round 2 expected late 2026. Up to $5,000 (Cert III), $6,000 (Diploma), $35,000 (ECT degree): 2026 scholarship program. See also all support programs (scholarships index) and Teach NSW get-paid-to-study. NSW early childhood careers hub.
  4. Commonwealth Prac Payment — $338.60/week (2026, means-tested, taxable) during mandatory teaching placements; apply via your university (SCU example).
  5. Casual/part-time educator while studying (agencies below place casuals with no minimum weekly commitment).
  6. Traineeships: Cert III Early Childhood traineeship = employed + paid while training (ask large providers: Goodstart, G8, Affinity, KU, Big Fat Smile, Uniting).
  7. On ECT qualification + NESA conditional/provisional accreditation → ECT or preschool teacher roles, then full accreditation (Proficient Teacher) after supervised practice.

Pay

Educators (Cert III, Diploma) are paid under the Children's Services Award, restructured from 1 March 2026; ECTs under the Educational Services (Teachers) Award or an enterprise agreement. Services receiving the government's Worker Retention Payment (extended to June 2028) must pay above-award minimums. See Fair Work — award level for Cert III educators.

Verify current hourly rates in the Fair Work Ombudsman pay guides — third-party pay sites give conflicting figures for 2026.
