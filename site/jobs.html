Jobs & Employment

The path from training to a full-time role, plus where to find work.

Path to a job

The training → casual → full-time ladder:

  1. Working With Children Check (~$105/5yrs paid class) — do first: apply here.
  2. Enrol fee-free Cert III → mandatory placement (~160 hrs) doubles as a working interview; many students are hired casually by their placement centre.
  3. NSW ECEC Scholarship Program 2026 — ~2,000 scholarships (up to $27M): study support payments + completion/employment payment; covers VET and birth–5 ECT degrees: 2026 scholarship program. See also all support programs (scholarships index) and Teach NSW get-paid-to-study. NSW early childhood careers hub.
  4. Casual/part-time educator while studying (agencies below place casuals with no minimum weekly commitment).
  5. Traineeships: Cert III Early Childhood traineeship = employed + paid while training (ask large providers: Goodstart, G8, Affinity, KU, Big Fat Smile, Uniting).
  6. On ECT qualification + NESA conditional/provisional accreditation → ECT or preschool teacher roles, then full accreditation (Proficient Teacher) after supervised practice.
