Your Early Childhood Teaching Career, NSW

A personalised hub for planning your return to work and your path to becoming an Early Childhood Teacher.

Welcome. This hub is built around your situation: you are 31, returning to work after around ten years raising your children, you taught young children at school in Pakistan, and you hold a Master in English Literature. That Master's degree is not itself a recognised Australian teaching qualification — but if universities assess it as at least bachelor-equivalent, it unlocks the faster graduate-entry teaching routes that career changers without a degree cannot take. Verify each university assesses overseas degrees itself; confirm with your chosen provider's admissions team. Your overseas teaching experience will strengthen your applications and interviews.

As an Australian citizen you are eligible for fee-free TAFE (Smart & Skilled), Commonwealth supported places (CSP) at university, and HECS-HELP to defer your student contribution — so the graduate-entry teaching route can cost very little upfront.

Below is the roadmap recommended for you, followed by links to the detailed sections of this hub.

Your Recommended Roadmap

  1. 1
    This month

    Apply for a Working With Children Check. Also complete an approved first aid course that includes asthma and anaphylaxis training (e.g. HLTAID012) — most services require it before you start. Verify whether a free volunteer check covers student placements, and whether paid work needs the paid check.

  2. 2
    This month

    Enrol in the fee-free Certificate III in Early Childhood Education and Care (TAFE NSW / Smart & Skilled) — online mode with local placement.

    Verify this step may be optional: under the National Regulations, someone actively working towards an approved qualification (which can include an ECT degree) may count as Certificate III-qualified for ratios. If so, you could start the Grad Dip/MTeach and work as an educator without the Cert III. Confirm with ACECQA and prospective employers.

  3. 3
    In parallel

    Apply for a Graduate Diploma of Early Childhood Education or Master of Teaching (Birth–5) intake, using the Master in English Literature as the entry degree, and apply for the current NSW ECEC Scholarship Program round.

    Verify 2027 intake dates and the current scholarship round (the 2026 round may have closed); English-language entry requirements (teaching degrees often ask for IELTS 7.5 or equivalent — ask whether your Master's in English counts as evidence); and whether the program requires LANTITE (the literacy and numeracy test for teaching students).

  4. 4
    Within ~6 months

    Use the Cert III placement (~160 hrs) to get hired casually; start earning while studying.

    Verify the Commonwealth Prac Payment (a means-tested weekly payment during mandatory teaching placements, introduced 2025) — check eligibility and the current rate.

  5. 5
    1.5–2 years

    Complete the ECT qualification part-time while working; obtain NESA conditional then provisional accreditation.

  6. 6

    Move into a full-time Early Childhood Teacher role (teacher salary band) and work toward Proficient Teacher accreditation.

    Verify current pay rates for Cert III educators, Diploma educators and ECTs (the award plus the government-funded wage increases from Dec 2024 and Dec 2025) before comparing pathways.
