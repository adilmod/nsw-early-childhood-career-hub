Talking about a career break

  • Name it once, briefly, and move on. “From [year] to [year] I was a full-time parent. I am now studying [course] and returning to work in early childhood.”
  • Turn it into evidence. Years of daily care map to EYLF Outcome 3 (wellbeing), routines, transitions, settling, and partnering with other families and schools (NQS QA6).
  • Show you are current. List your enrolment, Working With Children Check, first aid, and any child protection or child safety training you have completed.
  • Overseas experience counts. Describe the age group, class size, what you planned and taught, and what you would do differently under the EYLF.
  • Languages are a strength. Community languages help services support children with English as an additional language and their families.

Kind regards, [Name] [Phone] | [Email]

Documents checklist

  • Working With Children Check number (upgrade from volunteer to paid before starting paid work)
  • First aid certificate including asthma and anaphylaxis
  • Proof of enrolment / statement of attainment for your current course
  • Overseas degree certificate and transcript (and any assessment your university provided)
  • Two referees, ideally including a placement supervisor
