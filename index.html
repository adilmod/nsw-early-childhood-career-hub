Your Early Childhood Teaching Career, NSW

A personalised hub for planning your return to work and your path to becoming an Early Childhood Teacher.

Welcome. This hub is built around your situation: you are 31, returning to work after around ten years raising your children, you taught young children at school in Pakistan, and you hold a Master in English Literature. That Master's degree is not itself a recognised Australian teaching qualification — but because it is a bachelor-equivalent degree, it unlocks the faster graduate-entry teaching routes that career changers without a degree cannot take. Your overseas teaching experience will strengthen your applications and interviews.

Below is the roadmap recommended for you, followed by links to the detailed sections of this hub.

Your Recommended Roadmap

  1. 1
    This month

    Apply for a Working With Children Check.

  2. 2
    This month

    Enrol in the fee-free Certificate III in Early Childhood Education and Care (TAFE NSW / Smart & Skilled) — online mode with local placement.

  3. 3
    In parallel

    Apply for a Graduate Diploma of Early Childhood Education or Master of Teaching (Birth–5) intake, using the Master in English Literature as the entry degree, and apply for the NSW 2026 ECEC Scholarship Program.

  4. 4
    Within ~6 months

    Use the Cert III placement (~160 hrs) to get hired casually; start earning while studying.

  5. 5
    1.5–2 years

    Complete the ECT qualification part-time while working; obtain NESA conditional then provisional accreditation.

  6. 6

    Move into a full-time Early Childhood Teacher role (teacher salary band) and work toward Proficient Teacher accreditation.
